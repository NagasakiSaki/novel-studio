# Canon protection

Unconfirmed ideas, inferred motives and future outlines are not past facts. Keep source chapter/version or author confirmation with every proposed continuity update. Separate author truth, character knowledge and reader revelation.

Never silently revise canon or confirmed prose. Agent workspaces contain disposable drafts, reports and immutable source copies. Canonical is outside both active workspaces and explicitly denied by generated session rules. The separate author tool defaults to dry-run and requires the exact reviewed plan digest before promotion, with source/target revision checks, backups, an author-tool lock and atomic publication. These mechanisms do not isolate arbitrary same-user processes; a prompt is not filesystem isolation.

Confirmed prose, world canon, character records and important continuity records require explicit human approval for each scoped change. Editorial suggestions are not approval. Git and snapshots support recovery; they do not prevent a live write. A CLI session permission profile must be explicitly loaded; installing this plugin does not apply it automatically. Do not remove deny rules or use another tool to work around a refusal.
