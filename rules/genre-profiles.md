# Genre profiles

Ask for the work's literary contract when needed. Realism may rely on social causality and daily perception; mystery on information and reveal order; political science fiction on institutional and technological constraints; psychological fiction on limited understanding; experimental/non-linear fiction on an intentional relation between story time and telling order.

These are considerations, not compulsory plot formulas. Do not assign the same event density, moral resolution, voice or cliffhanger frequency to all genres.
