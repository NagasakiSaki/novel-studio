# Community conduct

Discuss creative and technical choices respectfully. Critique texts and evidence without personal attacks, harassment or discrimination. Respect author boundaries, privacy, mature literary topics and copyright.

Never post another person's manuscript, private correspondence or credentials without authorization. Use synthetic examples for bug reports. Do not promote account abuse, proxy/relay services or attempts to evade provider rules.

Project maintainers may remove harmful material and restrict participation. A private contact and enforcement process must be established before the public community launch.
